While I remember listening to some songs from Geese's 3D country in 2024, mostly in passing at the time sadly, it was really the beginning of 2025 that I started paying attention to their frontman Cameron Winter. As I have a perfectly curated TikTok algorithm, Love Takes Miles kept appearing right around the time it was released, and as is typical for me, I took my sweet time and only really listened to the album's hit single a handful of times before getting it. LastFM says I listened to the song 8 times from January to June, before completely stopping until October.
