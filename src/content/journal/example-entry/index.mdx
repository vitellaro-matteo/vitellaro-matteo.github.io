While I remember listening to some songs from Geese's _3D country_ in 2024, mostly passively at the time sadly, it was really the beginning of 2025 that I started paying attention to their frontman Cameron Winter. As I have a perfectly tailored TikTok algorithm, _Love Takes Miles_ kept appearing right around the time it was released, and as is typical for me, I took my sweet time and only really listened to the album's hit single a handful of times before getting it. According to LastFM, it was exactly 8 times from January to June, before completely stopping until October. 

<Track id="2zf1izCOz2F22PF27uhxRF" title="Love Takes Miles" artist="Cameron Winter" label="music" />

Now, October of 2025 was around the time I moved back in with my parents from my university housing in Luxembourg. I still had one semester ahead of me and spent it mostly in front of my PC coding, in my parents' kitchen cooking, and meeting old friends from my high school years. I also had the misfortune of reinjuring a cracked disc in my lower back, which sadly made me stop from visiting the gym until February of the next year. (I was able to look this up in my fitness application that I use to enter the gym, and checking the monthly entries, it's incredible how much my life is tracked on apps out there). As such, day-to-day life, although academically productive and culinarily fulfilling, felt from time to time difficult and isolating. Now I don't want this entry to be a story about my struggles with self-imposed pressure or doubts that come with it, but I believe that this backdrop serves to show my deep appreciation for Cameron Winter's work.
